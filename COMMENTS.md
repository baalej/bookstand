## 0.1

### No shadow is casted on its own page 

I have noticed one error or inconsistency in the way the shadows are being casted. I have identified two issues the lack of a shadow in the page itself that is being folded and the sharp shape, with no blur, of the shadow being casted into the opposite page when a page flip is taking place.

Regarding the first issue, we managed to improve the shadow being casted on the page that was being flipped, specially in the so called dog ear. The curve where the page is being folded now casts a shadow making it visible and natural. The problem here is that as we pull longer, in order to do a page flip, the corver whe are pulling is not casting a shadow itself, at least not on its own page, although as as soon as it crosses the spine or center of the book and towards the opposite page a shadow appears magically starting from the center line of the book (spread) casting the whole page we are pulling onto the one we are leaving. 

I have noticed the same shadow that is being casted on the opposite page of the one we are pulling is not casted when we pull from the bottom corners.

In conclusion to this issue, we need a consistent applicating of shadows that mimmicks those of the natural book. We have to do so while addressing obvious concerns about performance. We expect this library to be used multiple times accross the same page for displaying the content of a book on shops or portfolios, therefore we need to find the right balance on performance and realism.

### The shadow page lacks blur or natural shape

Another issue is the shape of the shadow. The same shadow we are talking about above that is being casted on the opposite page but not the page itself. That same shadow that only appears after we crossed the middle of the spread looks sharp and unnatural, lacking any blur soft lightning present on the other shadows.
